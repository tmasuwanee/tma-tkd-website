Where everything lives in the new system

One place to look up any person who touches TMA. Every lead, family, order, and form has one home now, so nobody gets cold-called after they have already signed up, and every alert points you to the right screen.

Where do I look?

Start here. Pick the row that matches the person in front of you.

Prospects

Someone interested, not signed up yet

Use when a new person wants info or a trial, or you need to call people back and book them in.

Holds: web inquiries, tour requests, Facebook leads, $99 & $49 trials. This is the call list and the calendar.

Enrolled Families

Already a paying student

Use for current members: belts, attendance, after-school enrolled families, contact info.

Holds: the student roster and after-school enrollments. These never show up in the call list.

Orders

Bought gear or a package

Use when someone paid for pro-shop items, a bundle, or a sale and you need to fulfill it.

Holds: pro-shop and seasonal sale orders. A purchase, not a person to chase.

Camp

Summer / seasonal camp

Use for camp registrations and camp field-trip payments.

Holds: camp registrations (already kept separate today, the model for the rest).

Forms & Calls

Paperwork and phone history

Use to find a signed waiver, a transportation form, or a call recording.

Holds: waivers, transportation forms, the voice-agent call log, and the test-call tool.

What is changing

Same information, organized so it is obvious who is who.

Before · everything in one pile

Every type of person landed in the same lead list.

  • Pro-shop buyers and enrolled families showed up in the "call these new leads" list.
  • Staff could not tell a hot prospect from a paying customer at a glance.
  • Emails went out as "TMA Summer Camp" even to martial-arts families.
  • The same person could appear in two or three places with no clear home.
  • Follow-ups fired from two different systems, sometimes twice.

After · one home each

Five clear sections, driven by a record type on every entry.

  • The call list shows only real prospects. Customers never resurface there.
  • Prospects, Enrolled, Orders, Camp, and Forms each have their own screen.
  • All email comes from one identity: Top Martial Arts <hello@tmatkd.com>.
  • Every alert names the real source and links to the right section.
  • Each automation has a single owner, so nothing double-fires.

Behind the scenes

What the system now does for you automatically, without stepping on itself.

One sender, correct labels

All email goes out as Top Martial Arts. Staff alerts and Telegram pings name the real program, not a generic title.

Prospects only on the call board

Orders and enrolled families are filtered out, so "call fast" never points at someone who already paid.

One owner per automation

Reminders, no-show recovery, and call alerts each run from a single place, so nobody gets contacted twice.

No duplicate people

Submitting a form twice updates the same record instead of creating a second one to clean up later.
